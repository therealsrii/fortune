I'm Sri, a mechanical engineer by trade and a PhD candidate in the School of Biomedical Engineering. My research sits at the intersection of semiconductors, biology and fluid dynamics, aimed at critical and pressing problems in healthcare.

I'm curious about a lot of things, but I keep coming back to counterintuitive ideas and trends in science and technology: the places where the data says something different from the story everyone tells. I like finding those numbers, charting them, and writing about what they mean.

Most of what I write here is commentary in that spirit. Why a breakthrough can still lose to a factory that was paid off decades ago. What happens when data itself becomes a commodity. How regulation can make it harder, not easier, to unseat the incumbents. My own work helps me read these stories: I work on fluid flow from microlitres to hundreds of litres a minute, across healthcare, critical minerals, agriculture and biomanufacturing, so I see up close where good science meets cost, scale and the market.
