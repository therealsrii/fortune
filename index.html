I'm Sri, a mechanical engineer by trade and a PhD candidate in the School of Biomedical Engineering at UBC. My research sits at the intersection of semiconductors, biology and fluid dynamics, aimed at critical and pressing problems in healthcare.

I'm curious about a lot of things, but I keep coming back to counterintuitive ideas and trends in science and technology: the places where the data says something different from the story everyone tells. I like finding those numbers, charting them, and writing about what they mean.

I like to look past the headline at what sits underneath it: the costs behind a breakthrough, the incentives behind a market, the physics behind a scale-up. That's usually where the surprises are. My own work helps. I work on fluid flow from microlitres to hundreds of litres a minute, across healthcare, critical minerals, agriculture and biomanufacturing, so I see up close where good science meets cost, scale and the market.
